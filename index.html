01

What is OPTIMA?

An IMI/EU consortium building an AI-driven data platform to improve guideline-based decision support for prostate, breast and lung cancer, combining real-world data, guideline logic and federated AI.

  • Coordinated by EAU & Pfizer, ~30 partners (academia, EFPIA industry, SMEs)
  • Core output: a platform unifying data, guidelines and AI models for clinicians
  • Runs 60 months across 9 Work Packages (WP1-WP9)
02

Why this matters

Clinicians

Near-live, guideline-based recommendations at the point of care

Patients

Better-informed, individualised treatment decisions

Research

Federated learning avoids moving sensitive patient data

Regulators/Payers

Real-world evidence to support guidelines and reimbursement
03

Platform architecture (WP4/WP5)

  • WP4 (ITTM): technical/infrastructural requirements, DMP, security-by-design
  • WP5 (EISBM): platform implementation, deployment and evaluation
  • Modular design: integration layer separated from decision/compute engines
  • Interoperability via HL7 FHIR, SNOMED-CT ontologies, EHR connectivity
04

Where my role fits

As Software Development Lead, I sit at the intersection of WP3 (guideline tool), WP4 (architecture) and WP5 (deployment) - turning existing components into one coherent, user-centred product.

  • Unify Smart Reporting, guideline engine and data platform via FHIR
  • Bridge clinical, legal (WP2) and technical stakeholders
  • Own delivery of new platform components end-to-end
